**The puzzle to keep in mind.** Her first paper is a vision paper (P01). Her 2015–2016 first-author papers are about visuomotor policies, inverse RL, and video prediction. Yet the thesis is almost entirely about meta-learning. Weeks 1–5 follow the story in the order it happened, and Week 6 compares it against the story the thesis tells.

## One hypothesis to test

*Her PhD can be read as a sequence of attempts to cut how much human supervision a robot needs for each new task.* Treat this as a hypothesis, not a premise. In Week 1, note a few concrete kinds of human input P02 needs, with section references. Revisit the idea in later memos when evidence changes it, then give a verdict in the final story.

The separate supervision ledger is optional. A few supporting and contradicting examples in the memos are enough; no paper-by-supervision matrix is required.

## Completion rule

Read, write the memo, and move on. Choose zero or one optional deep dive within the writing budget. Empty tracker files, unanswered extra prompts, and unverified details marked `[Q]` are acceptable. Profile, separate lessons, and `comparisons.md` are optional extracts after the final story.
