**The puzzle to keep in mind.** Her first paper is a vision paper (P01). Her 2015–2016 first-author papers are about visuomotor policies, inverse RL, and video prediction. Yet the thesis is almost entirely about meta-learning. Weeks 1–5 follow the story in the order it happened, and Week 6 compares it against the story the thesis tells.

## Researcher-specific tracker: the supervision ledger

[`supervision-ledger.md`](supervision-ledger.md) tests one **hypothesis** across all six weeks: *her PhD can be read as a sequence of attempts to cut how much human supervision a robot needs for each new task.* You'll set up the ledger in Week 1, extend it each week, and decide in Week 6 whether the hypothesis holds or is too neat. Don't assume it's true.
