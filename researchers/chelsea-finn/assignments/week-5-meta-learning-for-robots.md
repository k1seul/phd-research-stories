## Required: one short memo

Read the core, supporting, and context papers above. Separate paper notes are optional.

Write `synthesis/phase-5-meta-learning-for-robots.md` ([short template](../../../templates/phase-synthesis.md)):

1. **Earlier threads return.** Choose 2–3 examples from this week's papers showing how meta-learning revisits visuomotor learning, costs/IRL, or prediction/planning. Say what actually changes, with paper IDs. A full seven-paper matrix is unnecessary.
2. **Program or series?** What makes these papers look like a research program, or what argues against that reading? Include one observation about collaboration or the technical toolbox if it matters.
3. **Hypothesis update.** Revisit the Week-2 prediction about learned costs or the supervision hypothesis. What was reduced, what new assumptions appeared, and what remains uncertain?

There is no word minimum. Tag important story claims `[F]`, `[I]`, or `[Q]`. Spend **60–90 minutes total on organizing and writing**, including any optional work.

## Optional deep dive: choose zero or one
